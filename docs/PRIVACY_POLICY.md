Naw Tin ("the app") is a board game you can play against the computer or with a
friend on one device. This policy explains what information the app uses.

## Information we do not collect

- The app has **no accounts** and no sign-in.
- We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts,
  photos, files, precise or approximate location, or microphone or camera data.
- We do **not** run our own servers, analytics or tracking for the app.
- We do not sell your information.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves the following **only on your device**. It is never sent to us:

- your settings (sound, vibration, language, motion and power options);
- your last game choices (difficulty, who goes first);
- player names you type for a two-player game;
- your game statistics (for example wins, losses and tokens eaten).

You can clear the statistics in the app, and all of this data is removed when you
uninstall the app.

## Advertising

The app shows **optional rewarded video ads** (you choose to watch one to unlock
a hint or a rewind). Ads are provided by **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure
ads, AdMob and Google may collect and use information such as your device's
**advertising ID**, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions.
This happens through Google's software in the app, under Google's own policies:

- Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from apps that use its services:
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your
device settings (on Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

## Children

The app is **not directed to children under 13** (or the minimum age in your
country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

## Security

Data stored by the app stays in the app's private storage on your device. Ad
requests are sent over encrypted connections by the ad SDK.

## Your choices and rights

Because we do not collect or hold your personal information, there is nothing
for us to access, correct or delete. For data held by Google, use Google's own
privacy controls linked above.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The date at the top shows the
latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept it.
